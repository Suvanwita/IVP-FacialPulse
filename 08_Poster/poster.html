Clinical Motivation & The Diagnostic Gap
Psychiatric Need

The Depression Burden: Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) affects over 280 million individuals globally. Gold-standard screening relies on subjective self-report questionnaires (PHQ-9, BDI) and clinical interviews, which are hindered by:

  • Subjective Recall Bias & Social Desirability: Up to 50% of patients underreport emotional distress due to perceived stigma.
  • Passive Snapshot Fallacy: Conventional computer vision models examine static selfies or uncontrolled wild captures, suffering from natural resting morphological bias ("resting sad face").
  • Lack of Stimulus-Response Dynamic Testing: Psychiatric affective blunting manifests primarily during emotional induction and cognitive stress, not at static rest.
The FacialPulse Breakthrough: A standardized, interactive 43-second stimulus-response elicitation protocol combined with multimodal audio-visual fusion and baseline calibration—transforming consumer webcams into objective clinical screening tools.

FACS Action Units & Baseline Normalization
Phase 1 Vision Engine

Facial expressions are quantified using the Facial Action Coding System (FACS). To eliminate false positives stemming from invariant facial geometry, FacialPulse computes individualized differential activations:

Resting Baseline EMA Calibrator Formulation:
AUsmoothed(t) = α · AU(t) + (1 - α) · AUsmoothed(t - 1),   where α = 0.35

ΔAUi(t) = max(0,   AUi,smoothed(t) - AUi,baseline)
Action Unit (AU) Anatomical Muscle Psychiatric Significance in Depression
AU04 (Brow Lowerer) Corrugator Supercilii Persistent dwell reflects dysphoria, emotional pain & cognitive burden
AU15 (Lip Depressor) Depressor Anguli Oris Elevated resting & recovery dwell indicates sadness & negative affect
AU12 (Lip Puller) Zygomaticus Major Blunted amplitude & delayed latency characterize anhedonia
AU06 (Cheek Raiser) Orbicularis Oculi Duchenne Marker: AU06 + AU12 co-activation reveals genuine joy vs fake smile
AU01 (Inner Brow) Frontalis Pars Medialis Elevated in psychomotor distress and affective rigidity

Acoustic Biomarkers & Affective Concordance
Phase 3 Audio Engine

Vocal acoustic cues provide critical independent psychomotor markers. Depressed speech exhibits acoustic monotony, prolonged pause latencies, and reduced pitch dynamic range:

Pitch Dynamic Range (F0) Mean pitch, standard deviation, and semitone dispersion. Monotone prosody index ($MPI$) captures compressed frequency dynamic range.
Psychomotor Speech Retardation Voice-Activity Detection (VAD) identifies silent pauses. Pause ratio >45% and prolonged pause latencies (>750ms) mark psychomotor slowing.
Vocal Perturbation & Strain Local Jitter (pitch perturbation %) and Shimmer (amplitude perturbation dB) identify laryngeal muscle tension and vocal fatigue.
Masked Affect Detection Cross-modal concordance rate detects affective incongruence (e.g. forced social smile masking severe vocal psychomotor retardation).

Summary & Clinical Impact

FacialPulse addresses the fundamental limitations of subjective depression questionnaires through a guided, 43-second stimulus-response elicitation protocol that uncovers dynamic facial Action Unit blunting and acoustic speech retardation. Calibrated against the DAIC-WOZ benchmark, it delivers explainable DSM-5 symptom attributions and hospital-grade longitudinal tracking while strictly preserving patient privacy through zero-disk ephemeral RAM processing.
