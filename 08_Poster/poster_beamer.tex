    \begin{posterbox}{1. Clinical Motivation \& The Diagnostic Gap}
      \textbf{The Clinical Challenge:} Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) affects over 280 million people globally. Diagnostic reliance on self-report questionnaires (PHQ-9) suffers from:
      \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Subjective Recall Bias \& Stigma:} Reluctance to report affective distress.
        \item \textbf{Passive Snapshot Fallacy:} Invariant resting geometry ("resting sad face").
        \item \textbf{Absence of Dynamic Stressors:} Blunted affect appears under dynamic induction.
      \end{itemize}
      \vspace{0.2cm}
      \begin{tcolorbox}[colback=MedBlue!10,colframe=MedBlue,arc=1.5mm,boxrule=1pt]
        \small\textbf{FacialPulse Innovation:} A standardized 43-second active elicitation protocol combining facial Action Unit (FACS) tracking with acoustic speech prosody.
      \end{tcolorbox}
    \end{posterbox}

    \vspace{0.3cm}

    \begin{posterbox}{2. Standardized 4-Phase Elicitation Protocol}
      Guided 43-second clinical stimulus-response assessment:
      \begin{enumerate}
        \item \textbf{Phase 1: Baseline Calibration (8s):} Neutral gaze at screen; establishes individual morphological baseline offset.
        \item \textbf{Phase 2: Positive Affect Elicitation (12s):} Humorous stimulus; measures peak smile intensity, onset latency, and \textbf{Duchenne marker} (AU06 + AU12).
        \item \textbf{Phase 3: Cognitive Reading Task (15s):} Reading the \textit{Rainbow Passage} aloud; measures downward tilt, AU04 brow furrow, $F_0$ pitch std, and pause ratio.
        \item \textbf{Phase 4: Affective Recovery (8s):} Post-stimulus hold; measures emotional flexibility and residual brow/lip holding.
      \end{enumerate}
    \end{posterbox}

    \vspace{0.3cm}

    \begin{posterbox}{6. Acoustic Biomarkers \& Masked Affect}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Pitch Dynamics ($F_0$):} Standard deviation and semitone dispersion quantify prosodic flatlining and monotony.
        \item \textbf{Psychomotor Retardation:} Voice-Activity Detection measures pause ratios ($>45\%$) and extended silence ($>750$ ms).
        \item \textbf{Masked Affect Concordance:} Cross-modal discordance flags smiling depression (smiling presentation with severe vocal retardation).
      \end{itemize}
